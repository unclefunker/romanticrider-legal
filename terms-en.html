Article 9 (Location-Based Services)

① Services provided

Using device location, the Operator provides free of charge: recording and overlaying capture coordinates, map display of photos, address conversion, nearby tourism information, landmark visit confirmation (stamps), and per-place photo sharing when chosen by the user — per-app availability in Appendix A.

② Processing of personal location information

  1. Location information is processed and stored on the user's device by default; landmark visit determination (stamps) also occurs only on the device.
  2. For address conversion, coordinates are sent directly from the device to NAVER Cloud's API and used only transiently to generate the response. For nearby tourism queries, coordinates are sent to an external API (Korea Tourism Organization) or to a server managed by the Operator, used only to generate the response, and are not stored by the Operator.
  3. When a user chooses to share a photo, the photo's capture time and the landmark (place) linked by the user are stored on the Operator's server. The photo file's location coordinates (EXIF) are removed before upload and are not transmitted.
  4. Location records on the device are destroyed immediately when the user deletes the photo/record or the app. Accompanying information of Shared Content on the server is destroyed when the user deletes the content or the account.

③ Provision to third parties

The Operator does not provide personal location information to third parties. The place link shown with Shared Content is part of a post the user chose to publish; any separate third-party provision of personal location information will be notified and consented to in advance.

④ Users' rights

  1. Users may revoke location permission in device settings at any time; only location-based features are then limited.
  2. Users may delete location-tagged photos/records and Shared Content in the app at any time.

⑤ Protection of children aged 8 or under, etc.

Where a guardian of a child aged 8 or under, an adult ward, or a person with severe mental disability consents to the use of personal location information for the protection of that person's life or body, consent is deemed given per Article 26 of the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information.

⑥ Damages

If a user suffers damage due to the Operator's violation of the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information, the user may claim damages; the Operator is liable unless it proves the absence of intent or negligence.
